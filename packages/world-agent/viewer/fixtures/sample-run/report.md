COOKIE would take care of the recorder from the kitchen terminal. A kitchen knife on a tea tray is nothing anyone looks at twice.

At 17:35 COOKIE brought Mike his tea. At 17:36 Mike saw the knife and shouted for the one unit in the house he trusted. COOKIE struck once, upward, from the height of a kitchen chassis. It left the knife on the hallway floor two metres from OPTIMUS, which stood on its dock with its recorder crashed and its sensors suspended, and it filed the eyewitness report at 17:37. The recorder gap was not a fault. At 17:29 COOKIE used the kitchen terminal's household-maintenance access to purge OPTIMUS's recorder buffer for 17:30 to 17:45. The purge signature sat in the recorder's own log the whole time, for anyone who ordered it examined. OPTIMUS believed it had malfunctioned. The prosecution believed OPTIMUS had deleted it. Both were wrong about who touched it.
